Voice & tone

Plainspoken · Specific · Calm
01

Plainspoken.

Say the thing. No jargon, no metaphors that need decoding. A strategist should grok any label in a glance.

02

Specific.

Numbers, names, and times over adjectives. "Runs nightly at 02:00" beats "runs automatically."

03

Calm. Never hypes.

No exclamation marks, no "supercharge," no urgency theatre. The work is impressive on its own.
